My sensing research spans two complementary projects:

- **[Mapping the Physical Environment](/projects/sensing-place-physical/)** — measuring land cover and land-use change from crowdsourced geographic data and geo-tagged imagery.
- **[Measuring Urban Function & Deprivation](/projects/sensing-place-function/)** — understanding how places are used and where disadvantage is concentrated through landscape form and human activity.
